Does your motor stop without warning?
A motor that stops unexpectedly, changes speed erratically or keeps triggering drive faults can halt production. Running it without finding the cause tends to make things worse.
Why does proper drive troubleshooting matter?
A variable frequency drive controls motor speed by adjusting the frequency and voltage of the electrical supply. Troubleshooting helps pin down whether the problem is an electrical fault, a configuration issue or something in the connected equipment.
When should you bring in professional repair?
Repeated faults, overheating, strange noises or error codes that won't clear all need proper investigation. A qualified technician can assess the drive and tell you whether repair or replacement makes more sense.
